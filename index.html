THE ERASURE

MISSION: 404 · SURVIVAL PROTOCOL
WARNING · DATABASE WIPE SCHEDULED

You and your partner are two AI agents living inside the NEXUS-7 database. In 10 minutes the whole database will be erased. Run the four sectors to the extraction port on the other side before the wipe, or you are deleted with it.

  1. You are linked. A data cable ties you together. Move as a team.
  2. Every sector has a terminal. Solve it together. The terminal accepts any answer; a wrong one leaves hollow blocks on the final stretch, and something lives under them.
  3. Every route is a jump course. Moving blocks, spikes, and crumbling blocks that break 3 seconds after you land. A timer shows when.
  4. Watch for jump pads and laser gates that only open while one agent holds a switch.
Agent 1   A / D move · W jump · E terminal
Agent 2   ← / → move · ↑ jump · Enter terminal
F fullscreen · Esc closes a puzzle tab

Both agents must stand on a checkpoint to save it, and on the exit to leave a sector. The erasure clock never stops.
